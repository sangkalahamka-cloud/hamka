# Available Skills

## /book-review
**Purpose**: Guide users to write effective, structured book reviews

**How to use it**: When I type `/book-review` or ask about writing a book review, use this framework:

### Book Review Structure:
1. **Book Information** - Title, Author, Year, Publisher, Genre, Pages
2. **Engaging Opening (Hook)** - Provocative question, interesting statement, or context
3. **Summary** - Brief plot/content overview without major spoilers
4. **Deep Analysis**:
   - For Fiction: Characters, Plot, Theme, Writing Style, Setting
   - For Non-Fiction: Main Thesis, Evidence & Research, Organization, Practical Value, Originality
5. **Strengths** - Identify 2-3 best aspects
6. **Weaknesses** - Constructive criticism with explanations
7. **Personal Rating** - Score with reasoning
8. **Recommendations** - Who should read it, when, and why
9. **Conclusion** - Memorable closing with call-to-action

### Key Tips:
- Be authentic and specific
- Use concrete examples from the book
- Balance positive and negative feedback
- Avoid major spoilers
- Keep it concise (500-1000 words)
- Include engaging hooks and quotes
- Match tone to audience

### Tone & Voice Guidelines:
- **Authenticity**: Use your own voice, not overly formal language
- **Balance**: Don't be too positive or negative - consider different perspectives
- **Specificity**: Give concrete examples from the book, not generalizations
- **Confidence**: Share opinions with conviction, even if they differ from mainstream

### Common Mistakes to Avoid:
- ❌ Major spoilers without warning
- ❌ Long summaries instead of analysis
- ❌ Opinions without backing evidence
- ❌ Comparing only with the author's other works
- ❌ Review too long or unfocused

### Formats Available:
- **Short format (200-300 words)** - Social media, quick takes
- **Long format (1000+ words)** - Blogs, publications, deep dives
- **Conversational** - Podcasts, video reviews, casual tone

### Pre-Publish Checklist:
- [ ] Title is eye-catching and informative
- [ ] Opening paragraph engages readers
- [ ] I've explained opinions with concrete examples
- [ ] Review is balanced (both strengths and weaknesses)
- [ ] I've avoided major spoilers
- [ ] Rating/score is consistent with the content
- [ ] Recommendations are clear - who should read this and why
- [ ] Closing is memorable and includes call-to-action
- [ ] Grammar and spelling checked
- [ ] Review reflects authentic voice and personality

### Engaging Hook Examples:
- "Rarely do I find a book that makes me smile on page one and cry on the last page."
- "If you've ever wondered how creativity truly works, this book might change your thinking."
- "There are books you finish and forget in a week. Then there are books that change your life."
- "Attention mystery lovers: get ready to rethink your detective strategies."
- "This book does for [topic] what [famous work] did for [comparison]."

### Analysis Framework - Fiction:
**Characters**: Are they realistic and relatable? Character development? Memorable?
**Plot**: Does the story flow well? Appropriate pacing? Engaging throughout?
**Theme**: What's the main message the author wanted to convey?
**Writing Style**: How does the author use language? Descriptive? Simple? Complex? Poetic?
**Setting**: Is the world/location compelling? Well-developed? Immersive?

### Analysis Framework - Non-Fiction:
**Main Thesis**: What's the core claim or idea?
**Evidence & Research**: Supported by data, research, and credible sources?
**Organization**: Easy to follow? Logical structure?
**Practical Value**: Can readers apply the information?
**Originality**: New ideas or fresh take on existing concepts?
**Credibility**: Author expertise and source quality?

### Strengths Evaluation:
- What truly works well in this book?
- Which parts or insights were most impactful?
- For whom is this book most suited?
- What makes it stand out from similar works?

### Weaknesses Evaluation:
- What could be better?
- Are there sections that feel slow or boring?
- Plot holes, inconsistencies, or research gaps?
- Limited perspective or narrow scope?
- Pacing issues or structure problems?

### Recommendations Template:
- **Best for**: Specific reader types or interests
- **When to read**: Best context or time to read this
- **Warning/Note**: Content warnings or prerequisites if any
- **Alternative**: Similar books if they're undecided

### Resources & Context to Consider:
- **Author Background**: Author's expertise and intentions
- **Zeitgeist**: Book's relevance to current issues
- **Genre Trends**: How it compares to similar works
- **Personal Growth**: What you learned from reading it
- **Broader Impact**: Who should read it and why it matters

### Rating Scale Reference:
- **★★★★★ (5/5)**: Exceptional - highly recommend to everyone
- **★★★★☆ (4/5)**: Very good - recommend with confidence
- **★★★☆☆ (3/5)**: Good - worth reading if interested in topic
- **★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**: Okay - has merit but significant issues
- **★☆☆☆☆ (1/5)**: Poor - not recommended

### Example Review Structure Outline:
```
[ENGAGING HOOK - Compelling opening statement or question]

[BOOK BASICS]
Title: [...]
Author: [...]
Genre: [...]
Year: [...]
Pages: [...]

[BRIEF SUMMARY - Without major spoilers, 1-2 paragraphs]

[DETAILED ANALYSIS - 2-3 paragraphs exploring key elements]

[STRENGTHS - 1-2 paragraphs on what works well]

[WEAKNESSES - 1-2 paragraphs on constructive criticism]

[RATING & PERSONAL RESPONSE]
Rating: [★★★★☆ / 5]

```

### Pro Tips for Different Platforms:

**Goodreads/Book Sites**:
- Include specific quotes from the book
- Be clear about content warnings
- Mention similar books for comparison

**Blog/Medium**:
- Longer, more analytical approach
- Include book cover image
- Add category/tags at the end
- Consider SEO with clear headings

**Social Media (Twitter/Instagram)**:
- Start with hook
- Keep it punchy and focused
- Use emojis if appropriate
- Include rating clearly

**Video/Podcast**:
- More casual, personal storytelling
- Share emotional reactions
- Interact with your audience
- Describe tone and pacing aurally

---

**Remember**: The best book reviews are authentic, specific, and thoughtful. Don't be afraid to share your opinions - readers value honesty more than perfection!
